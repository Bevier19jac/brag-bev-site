Investor Brief · Dumpster Diver

The concept is proven.
Help us finish it.

Dumpster Diver is a self-righting, impact-activated foam pod that cleans a trash bin without hoses, scrubbing, or touching the bin. Independent lab testing has validated the actuation, the spray coverage, and the housing. One scoped engineering refinement stands between this prototype and a production-ready design.

The Last Mile — Stated Plainly

One item left: self-righting geometry

Testing isolated a single remaining refinement: the pod must settle upright consistently after the throw. Our mechanical engineering partner has already identified the correction — a fully rounded hemispherical base with a lowered center of gravity — and the next design iteration is scoped around solving and validating exactly that, with defined pass/fail drop-test criteria before any further lab spend.

We tell you this up front because that's how we run the company: real partners, real lab data, no inflated claims. An investor here isn't funding a question mark — they're funding a known fix with a validation gate.

Step 1

Complete the scoped base-geometry redesign with our engineering partner.

Step 2

Validate self-righting in-house with locked-actuation drop testing against pass/fail criteria.

Step 3

Confirm with a focused independent lab round, then move to production-ready design.

The Opportunity

A chore everyone hates.
A product no one has.

01

Universal problem

Nearly every household owns a trash bin, and nearly none get cleaned — the job is disgusting, awkward, and easy to put off forever.

02

No shelf competitor

Today's options are truck-based cleaning services or a hose and a brush. There is no toss-in, walk-away consumer product on shelves.

03

Consumable revenue

Each pod is single-use by design. Regular bin maintenance means repeat purchase — a recurring-revenue consumer product, not a one-time gadget.

04

Simple by design

No batteries, no electronics, no moving parts beyond the mechanism itself. Armed by a clamp, fired by gravity — cheap to make, easy to ship.
