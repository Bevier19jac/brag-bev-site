Dumpster Diver · Patent pending

The bin that cleans itself.

Pull the clip. Drop it in. Close the lid. The landing does all the work.

I

Every week. Same bin. Same smell.

Every house on the street rolls a bin to the curb. And every week, it comes back like this.

II

There's gotta be a better way.

I sketched it. I filed it. The first patent filing went in on November 24, 2024.

III

Then I called everyone.

I called every chemical engineering department at every university and college I could think of, and offered it as a capstone project for their students.

A lot of the professors couldn't take it on, but they pointed me in the right direction. All the way to scientists at Imperial College London and Warsaw University of Technology, getting the mechanics, the chemistry and the spray just right.

IV

The standard was three things.

  1. 1It has to right itself and trigger.
  2. 2It has to break up dirt and grime, with a pleasant-smelling critter deterrent.
  3. 3It has to give consistent coverage on the whole inside of the bin.
V

I built the wrong one a few times. Then I built this.

The egg tipped. The cylinder fizzled. The can launched. The one that works lands on a round, weighted base, rights itself, and fires on impact.

VI

Three steps. Then walk away.

No battery. No button. The landing does all the work.

It's one-time use, so you never have to reach in and grab it out.

So fresh, and so clean.
