How It Works

Three steps. Ten seconds.

1

Pull & toss

Pull the red C-clamp off the base — that opens the arming gap — and toss the pod into your empty bin.

2

Land & slam

The weighted base self-rights the pod — and the landing slams the housing down through the gap, firing the actuator.

3

Foam takes over

High-expansion foam erupts upward and clings to the walls — dissolving grime and neutralizing odor while you walk away.

The Mechanism

Armed by a clamp.
Fired by gravity.

A red C-clamp holds the housing locked to the weighted base — totally safe to handle and ship. Pull it off and a gap opens between housing and base. When the pod lands in your bin, the housing slams down through that gap and drives the actuator: the can fires. Simple physics, no batteries, no buttons.
